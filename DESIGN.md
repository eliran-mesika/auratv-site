# Aura TV Site Design Contract

- Keep this repo website-only. Do not modify the iOS app source or app screenshots from this site repo.
- Visual direction: bright premium utility for a player-only media app, with honest policy copy and actual app UI as proof.
- The brand icon, favicons, manifest icons, and social image must stay derived from the approved Aura TV app icon and `assets/og-image.png` contract in `AGENTS.md`.
- Navigation should expose App, Blog, Support, Privacy, and Terms where context allows. Homepage may include Screenshots anchors.
- Every marketing and blog surface must preserve the player-only claim: Aura TV does not include channels, playlists, broadcaster accounts, catalogs, sports packages, movies, or content access.
- Screenshots should remain inspectable and unblurred. They are product and App Review evidence, not generic decoration.
- Use `auratv@mesikalabs.com` for app support, `privacy@mesikalabs.com` for privacy, `billing@mesikalabs.com` for billing, and `legal@mesikalabs.com` for legal.
