An IPTV Player Is Not a Secret Channel Machine

Aura TV is an IPTV player. That means it can play playlists, credentials, and guide data you provide. It does not include channels, hidden catalogs, or a suspicious "trust me bro" entertainment vault. If someone promised you that, congratulations, you have met marketing with a fake mustache.

Bring your own playlist.

The normal setup path is simple: add your M3U playlist, Xtream-compatible credentials, or guide data from a provider you already use and have rights to access. Aura TV organizes playback. It does not sell or bundle the content.

Why the distinction matters.

Player-only apps are cleaner, easier to support, and less legally radioactive. You get a focused iOS player; you do not get a mystery box of channels from a server nobody wants to explain.
