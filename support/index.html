Aura TV support

Support

Player-only IPTV app for user-provided sources.

Support email: auratv@mesikalabs.com

Billing email: billing@mesikalabs.com

Response target: within 2 business days.

Account required: no first-party Aura TV account is required for the core player flow.

Apple platforms iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV where the app is available.
Android platforms Android phone/tablet, Android TV, and Google TV where the app is available.

What support can help with

  • Importing M3U playlist URLs, local M3U files, and Xtream-compatible credentials from sources you are authorized to use.
  • Guide data such as EPG or XMLTV when your source provides it.
  • Playback startup, device compatibility, local storage, and settings behavior.
  • Restore and subscription status through Apple App Store / StoreKit or Google Play Billing.
  • Privacy, policy, and store-review questions about Aura TV itself.

What support cannot provide

  • Channels, playlists, credentials, broadcaster accounts, movies, sports packages, catalogs, or content access.
  • Authorization to use a third-party source.
  • Recovery of third-party provider accounts, passwords, tokens, or subscriptions.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Source import fails: confirm the URL, file, host, username, and password are correct and authorized.
  • Guide data is missing: confirm your source includes an EPG/XMLTV feed and that the guide URL is reachable.
  • Playback does not start: check your network and whether the user-provided stream URL still works on that device.
  • Purchase state is missing: use the restore or manage subscription option for the platform where the purchase was made.

What to include in a support request

  • App version, platform, and device model.
  • Whether the issue affects import, guide data, playback, restore, or settings.
  • A redacted example of the setup flow or playlist format.
  • Do not send playlist passwords, provider credentials, private account details, tokens, or full paid source URLs in public channels.

Legal and policy

Aura TV is a player for user-provided sources. It does not provide channels, catalogs, credentials, playlists, or content access. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
